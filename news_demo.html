News events: flag icons

Economic-calendar events drawn with chart.addNewsEvents(). Real events from 3–14 July 2023 (UTC); the EURUSD prices are made up. The replay stops before Friday's US jobs report, so it shows as upcoming.

Chart Currencies Impact Replay
  • Hover an icon for its card; click to keep it open, click anywhere else to close it. On touch screens, tap. The card is plain page HTML built from the chart's hover and click events, the way an app would do it.
  • The outline color is the impact: red high, orange medium, blue low. A number means several events share the icon.
  • Faded icons have not happened yet: the card shows a countdown and keeps the actual value hidden until the replay passes them.
  • Zoom out and icons that would overlap merge into one.
